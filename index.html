Set it on your Galaxy Fold

Set the portrait and landscape views before you confirm.

  1. Open in Gallery

    Download your collage and open it in Samsung Gallery. Open the menu and choose Set as wallpaper.

  2. Position the portrait view

    Rotate your Fold to portrait orientation. Drag the wallpaper until the top, portrait section of the collage is correctly framed on the screen.

  3. Position the landscape view

    While still in the wallpaper preview, rotate your phone to landscape orientation. Now drag the wallpaper until the bottom, landscape section is correctly framed on the screen.

  4. Confirm with OK

    Once you have positioned both views, tap OK to apply your wallpaper.
